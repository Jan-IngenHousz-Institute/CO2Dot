## FreeCAD files made during the internship of P. Berkhout.

These are the Freecad/3mf files for the leaf chamber designed by Paul Berkhout during his internship at JII March-July 2026. 

# chamber_CO2Dot_openExit
Design of a leaf chamber for the CO2Dot with exit of the air not directed to tubing.
The chamber can be made close better by adding magnets (5x5x5 mm). See for example TRU COMPONENTS 505926.


# chamber_CO2DOt_tubingExit
Design of a leaf chamber for the CO2Dot with the air flow being directed to tubing. 
The chamber can be made close better by adding magnets (5x5x5 mm). See for example TRU COMPONENTS 505926.

# attachment_chamber_clip
Design for the "clothespin like" connector to pin together the opposite extremity of the cell.

# CO2Dot_mask
Mask for the CO2Dot, to expose in the chamber of the leaf cell only the BME and the spectrometer.  

# mold_gasket
Mold for the silicone gaskets.
